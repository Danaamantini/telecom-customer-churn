# Tableau executive dashboard

[`telecom_churn_analysis.twb`](telecom_churn_analysis.twb) is the canonical
Tableau workbook. [`telecom_churn_dashboard.png`](telecom_churn_dashboard.png)
is the exported executive view used in the main project README.

The dashboard is designed to answer four business questions in one page:

1. How large is churn and the monthly revenue associated with it?
2. Where is churn concentrated?
3. Which customer group is the clearest retention priority?
4. Which addressable populations should be tested first?

## Data source

The workbook uses the customer-level output generated by the pipeline:

```text
data/processed/clean_customers.csv
```

Run `python run_pipeline.py` before opening the workbook. If Tableau cannot
find the data, replace the connection with the processed CSV above.

`Joined` customers remain in the source but are excluded from churn-rate
denominators. In this project, **existing customers** means `Stayed + Churned`.

## Dashboard metrics

| Metric | Definition | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| Existing customers | Distinct customers where status is not `Joined` | 6,589 |
| Churned customers | Distinct customers where status is `Churned` | 1,869 |
| Churn rate | Churned customers / existing customers | 28.4% |
| Existing monthly revenue | Sum of monthly charge for existing customers | $428,487.25 |
| Churn-associated monthly revenue | Sum of monthly charge for churned customers | $137,086.65 |
| Churn-associated revenue share | Churn-associated / existing monthly revenue | 32.0% |
| Month-to-Month concentration | Month-to-Month churned / all churned | 88.6% |
| High-current-value exposure | Churn revenue from monthly charge >= $90.40 / all churn revenue | 40.4% |

The term **revenue associated with churn** is deliberate. The dataset does not
contain costs or margin, so this metric must not be described as profit lost.

## Contract comparison

| Population | Existing customers | Churned customers | Churn rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Month-to-Month | 3,202 | 1,655 | 51.7% |
| One Year and Two Year combined | 3,387 | 214 | 6.3% |

The bases differ by only 185 customers, but the observed churn counts differ
by 1,441. Contract choice is associated with churn; it is not proof that moving
a customer to a longer contract will cause retention.

## Priority segment

The priority segment is the intersection of:

```text
Internet type = Fiber Optic
Contract = Month-to-Month
Tenure = 4 through 24 months, inclusive
Customer status = Stayed or Churned
```

It contains 807 existing customers, 496 churned customers, a 61.5% churn rate,
and $42,417.50 in churn-associated monthly revenue.

Reported churn categories are grouped for decision-making:

| Dashboard group | Source churn categories | Customers | Share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Competitor | Competitor | 239 | 48.2% |
| Service Experience | Dissatisfaction, Attitude | 175 | 35.3% |
| Price | Price | 43 | 8.7% |
| Other | All remaining categories | 39 | 7.9% |

## Recommended retention tests

The action cohorts overlap and therefore must not be added together.

| Test | Historical signal | Current addressable population |
|---|---|---|
| Redesign Offer E | 426 churns among 630 existing Offer E customers (67.6%) | 379 non-churned customers; $17,553.85 monthly revenue |
| Protect high-value Month-to-Month | Monthly charge >= $90.40 and Month-to-Month contract | 291 `Stayed` customers; $28,814.85 monthly revenue |
| Test proactive Fiber support | 438 churned customers with Fiber, Month-to-Month, tenure 4–24 months, and no premium tech support | 253 matching `Stayed` customers; $20,388.05 monthly revenue |

Each test needs a treatment group, a comparable control group, and predefined
outcomes such as churn and retained monthly revenue.

## Secondary geographic diagnostic

ZIP3 `921` contains 287 existing customers and 188 churns, a 65.5% churn rate.
Competitor reasons account for 153 of those churns, and 147 mention an offer.
This is a localized diagnostic signal, not evidence that location causes
churn. ZIP codes are identifiers and should remain text fields.

## Workbook contents

The final workbook contains:

- `QA - Core Metrics` for reconciliation;
- four KPI worksheets;
- `Contract Comparison`;
- `Priority Segment`;
- `Priority Churn Drivers`;
- `Executive Churn Dashboard`.

The QA worksheet is not part of the final dashboard. Its purpose is to confirm
the core totals before publishing.

## Refresh and export

1. Run `python run_pipeline.py`.
2. Open `dashboard/telecom_churn_analysis.twb` in Tableau Desktop or Tableau
   Public.
3. Refresh or replace the CSV connection if prompted.
4. Check the QA totals against the metric table above.
5. Open `Executive Churn Dashboard` and export it as PNG to
   `dashboard/telecom_churn_dashboard.png`.

The committed PNG is exported directly from Tableau. The optional
`src/dashboard_preview.py` utility is only a lightweight standalone preview and
does not replace the canonical Tableau export.
